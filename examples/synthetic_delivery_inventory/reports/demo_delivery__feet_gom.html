Coordinate system needs choosingDetails

In plain words: Several map projections fit the clues equally well, or the clues contradict the coordinates. Loading with the wrong one shifts the survey by metres to hundreds of kilometres.

Exactly: Candidates: EPSG:26715 (NAD27 / UTM zone 15N), EPSG:26716 (NAD27 / UTM zone 16N), EPSG:26717 (NAD27 / UTM zone 17N), EPSG:26714 (NAD27 / UTM zone 14N). Several CRSs fit equally well: EPSG:26715, EPSG:26716, EPSG:26717.

What to do: Choose the CRS with --crs EPSG:xxxx (or in the web app). Ask the data supplier if unsure.
