## Download

**Windows:** download `Segwise.exe` below and double-click it. Your browser opens with Segwise. Keep the small black window open while you use it; close it to quit.

The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new and not yet code-signed. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**Mac and Linux:** `pipx install "segwise[ui] @ git+https://github.com/gylshaurya/segwise"`, then run `segwise ui`.

Everything runs on your computer. Seismic files are read where they are and are never uploaded.
